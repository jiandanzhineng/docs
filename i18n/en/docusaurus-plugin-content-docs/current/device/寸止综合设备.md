## Safety

- The plug is not fully waterproof. Do not soak it. After cleaning, store it in a sealed bag so the outer skin does not crack.
- Do not use the pulse function on the heart, head, or torso, and do not let current pass through those areas.
- Voltage on the body must not exceed 36 V. Do not use it if you have a relevant medical condition or feel unwell.
