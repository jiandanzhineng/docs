# Solo Play Safety

Playing alone is freer, and it also means you are responsible for your own safety. Read this page before you start, especially the **auto lock** notes.

## Safety first: a way out

**Solo play requires a way out. If you do not have one, do not play alone.**

The devices depend on the network and software. Nobody can promise that nothing will fail: the network can drop, the program can stall, or a device can fault. If you are locked and cannot reach anyone, getting out is on you.

Keep a way out that does not depend on the device. For example, keep scissors where you can reach them and cut a rope if you have to.

> Do not put all your hope on "the device will unlock itself." That is a last resort, not an escape plan.

## Auto lock notes

The auto lock (simple smart lock terminal) is the device most often used for solo restraint. Know how it behaves:

1. **Low-battery unlock**: below 20% battery it unlocks, so you are not stuck. This is the last resort. Do not plan to rely on it.
2. **Hold the button to unlock**: hold the button on the lock for about **300 seconds** and it force-unlocks.
3. **After a force unlock, the lock cannot lock again**: once you force-unlock it, **that lock will not lock anymore**. Treat this as a last resort. After you use it, the lock needs factory repair.

> Before you use it, confirm you know where the button is and that your hand can reach it.

## Start the game with a button (solo restraint)

Button start is for playing alone: you can **lock yourself first, then press the button on the lock to start the game**. Nobody else has to tap Start.

Phone and PC use the same idea. The entry point differs.

### On the phone

1. Open the game you want and finish device mapping and parameters.
2. At the bottom of the config page, tap **Button start**.
3. In "Choose a button trigger", pick an **online device that has a button** (for example the auto lock).
4. Tap **Press once to test**, then actually press the button on the lock. **Button received** means the test passed.
5. Tap **Confirm**. You enter the "about to start" waiting page.
6. The game has not started yet. **Press the button on the lock once** and the game starts.

### On the computer

1. Open the game you want and finish device mapping and parameters.
2. At the bottom of the config page, next to **Start**, tap **Start from device button**.
3. In "Choose a button trigger", pick an **online device that has a button** (for example the auto lock).
4. Tap **Test once**, then actually press the button on the lock. **Button received** means the test passed.
5. Tap **Confirm**. You enter the "about to start" waiting page.
6. The game has not started yet. **Press the button on the lock once** and the game starts.

> The waiting page has Cancel and Force start. Cancel returns you without playing. Force start begins immediately if you do not want to wait for the button.
>
> You can confirm and enter the waiting page only after the button test passes. That avoids locking yourself and then finding that the button does nothing and the game will not start.
