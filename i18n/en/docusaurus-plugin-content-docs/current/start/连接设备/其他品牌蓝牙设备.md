# Third-Party Bluetooth Devices

Besides our own devices, the client also supports the third-party Bluetooth devices below. Third-party devices always connect over **Bluetooth direct** — never over WiFi/hotspot.

## Supported devices

"Verified" means it was tested on real hardware; "Theoretical" means the protocol is implemented and identified by broadcast name/service, but has not been verified on a real unit — individual models may not be recognized.

| Brand / Model | Display name in client | Capability | Phone | PC | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DG-LAB Coyote (D-LAB, COYOTE, ESTIM broadcast names) | Bluetooth estim device | Shock, waveform output | ✅ | ✅ | Verified |
| DG-LAB V3 (47L broadcast name) | Bluetooth estim device (direct) | Shock, waveform output | ✅ | ✅ | Verified |
| YCY shocker (YCY, YOKO, DJ-V2, etc.) | Shock device | Shock | ✅ | ✅ | Theoretical |
| YCY toys / motor class (FJB, TDD, etc.) | Motor device | Motor control | ✅ | ✅ | Theoretical |
| YCY masturbator (YCY-FJB-03, "cup" class) | Cup device | Motor, vibration, heating | ✅ | ✅ | Verified |
| YCY enema machine (ENEMA, GLJ, "enema" class) | Enema device | Pump | ✅ | ✅ | Theoretical |
| Fanye Bobobei (SOSEXY) | Bobobei | Vibration, suction, micro-current | ✅ | ✅ | Theoretical |

## Connect on the phone

1. Power on the device so it is in Bluetooth broadcast mode.
2. Open the client and tap "Add device" or the plus button at the top right — the client recognizes third-party devices by broadcast name automatically.
3. Pick the device and tap "Done". Added means connected.

## Connect on PC

Third-party devices are managed under "Device Management → Brand devices" in the PC client:

1. Make sure PC Bluetooth is on and the device is broadcasting.
2. Open "Device Management → Brand devices": auto-connect is on by default, so recognized devices connect as soon as they are scanned. You can also click scan manually and pick a device from the list.

## Notes

- **A heart-rate band only reports heart rate** — it has no controllable output and cannot be used as a stim/shock device.
- On PC, YCY devices support a bridge mode besides direct Bluetooth; Fanye Bobobei and GXP are direct-Bluetooth only.
- Shock devices (DG-LAB, YCY shocker) can be mapped as shock output in plays just like our own shock devices once connected.
