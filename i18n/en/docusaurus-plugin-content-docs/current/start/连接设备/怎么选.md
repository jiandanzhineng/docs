# Choosing a Connection Method

Devices support either Bluetooth or WiFi — only one at a time. After provisioning or joining a hotspot, the device turns Bluetooth off automatically; to go back to direct Bluetooth control, switch the device back to BLE mode.

| Method | Who it's for | Prerequisite | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Bluetooth direct (phone)](/docs/start/connect/ble) | Daily play on a phone | Device blinking blue, phone Bluetooth on | After provisioning or joining a hotspot the device turns Bluetooth off — switch it back to BLE mode |
| [PC hotspot](/docs/start/connect/hotspot) | Playing from the PC client | The PC can broadcast a 2.4G hotspot named `easysmart` with password `11111111` | The PC client can only control our own devices over WiFi/LAN; PC Bluetooth is only for [third-party devices](/docs/start/connect/brand-devices) (such as DG-LAB) |
| [Provision to home WiFi](/docs/start/connect/pc-home-wifi) (works from phone or PC) | The PC can't broadcast a hotspot, phone remote control, firmware upgrade, multiple devices online | A 2.4G WiFi at home and a router that supports mDNS | Provisioning only supports 2.4G WiFi; afterwards the device turns Bluetooth off |
| Phone hotspot (Android LAN service, method A) | No router — use an Android phone | Android phone with a hotspot named `easysmart`, password `11111111` | Android only; the device auto-connects to the gateway broker. iPhones can't use a fixed-name hotspot — provision through a router instead |

By default, devices automatically join the WiFi named `easysmart` with password `11111111`. If you don't want to provision, you can just use this default WiFi.

## Recommendations

- **Daily play on a phone**: use [Bluetooth direct](/docs/start/connect/ble) — power on and play.
- **Playing on a PC**: have the PC broadcast a [2.4G hotspot](/docs/start/connect/hotspot); if that's not possible, [provision to home WiFi](/docs/start/connect/pc-home-wifi).
- **Phone remote control, firmware upgrades, or multiple devices online**: provision, or use the phone's "LAN service" to put the devices on WiFi.
