# PC Hotspot (Connect the Device to a PC)

The PC client can only control our own devices over WiFi / the LAN — it cannot pair directly over Bluetooth like a phone. Bluetooth on PC is only for [third-party brand devices](/docs/start/connect/brand-devices) (such as DG-LAB). The easiest way is to have the PC broadcast a hotspot; the device connects automatically.

1. Turn on the PC hotspot with WiFi name `easysmart`, password `11111111`. The band must be **2.4G**.
2. If the device joins the WiFi but no device shows up in the client, turn off the PC firewall.
3. Power on the device. About 15~30 seconds after boot it connects to the PC automatically.

## Notes

- If the device previously joined a 5G WiFi, it may still be on the 5G band after you set 2.4G. In that case, let the device join a 2.4G WiFi once (or connect via Ethernet) and try again.
- By default, devices automatically join the WiFi named `easysmart` with password `11111111`. If you don't want to provision, you can just use this default WiFi.

If you can't run a PC hotspot, you can [provision the device to your home WiFi](/docs/start/connect/pc-home-wifi) and let the PC reach it over the LAN.
