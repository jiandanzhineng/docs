PC and phone both get a major feature: **remote device connection**. You can operate a device, or play along, without standing next to it. 🎉

<!-- truncate -->

## Remote connection

You used to have to stay next to the device. Not anymore:

- **Open a room in one tap:** put the device online and get a room code
- **Friends join with the code:** enter the code and operate your device remotely
- **Same as holding it:** device state stays in sync, and every action has feedback
- **Safer:** the transfer is encrypted. You can set an **intensity cap** and a **session length**. It stops when time runs out
- **One device, several people:** more than one person can operate it remotely at the same time

**PC and phone both support it.** You can open a room on the computer and have a friend join from a phone, or the other way around.

## Also new on PC

- **New look:** a dark tech style, with dark / light / auto
- **Easier flashing:** USB firmware flash. Plug in a data cable. It is faster, and it identifies the model and checks the driver
- **More ways to connect:** Bluetooth, in addition to the existing network modes
- **Automatic tests:** a calmer test page
- **Account login:** email sign-up and login, so a new computer does not mean setting everything up again
- **More games:** new plays, voices, and a device console

## Also new on phone

- **Remote connection:** same as PC. Open a room or join one, and operate a device remotely
- **Online games:** faster loading and a clearer screen
- **Device message log:** communication history in one place
- **Edging play:** clearer counts and status

Update the PC or phone client to the latest version to try it.
